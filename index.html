About Us

"Le Bon Lieu" — The Good Place

Lamine's brings together two worlds: the charm of a small Parisian café and the warmth of Afro-Caribbean home cooking. Guests describe it as feeling like a European escape — spacious, beautifully designed, and welcoming — while the kitchen turns out oxtails, jerk salmon, fufu, and plantain that taste like home.

Whether you're stopping in for a latte and a pastry or settling in for a full plate of Sunday-style comfort food, Lamine's is built to be exactly what the name promises: the good place.
